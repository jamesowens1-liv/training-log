Body scans

Where the weight went, February to August

Bioimpedance scales read body water and infer everything else from it. Test-to-test error for fat-free mass is commonly ±1–2 kg, and larger for individual limbs, so the 0.2 kg change in each arm is inside the noise while the 4 kg total change is probably real. Two other things move these numbers: daily creatine raises intracellular water and so props the lean figure up, and the shaded periods are the two Mounjaro courses.

Why the August scan showed muscle falling off a cliff

The scan headline says muscle dropped off a cliff in August. The segment readings on the same report say otherwise. Both lines below come from your own scans.

Headline figure on the scan Torso + both legs + both arms, added up

February

33.9 torso + 10.4 + 10.5 legs
+ 3.7 + 3.7 arms = 62.2 kg

Headline said 62.0 kg. The two agree.

August

31.7 torso + 9.7 + 9.8 legs
+ 3.5 + 3.5 arms = 58.2 kg

Headline said 34.6 kg. It cannot be both.

The August scan switched from reporting total muscle mass to skeletal muscle mass — a narrower measure that strips out organ and skin lean tissue. 34.6 kg is about 56% of your fat-free mass, which is what skeletal muscle mass normally comes out at. Nothing dropped 27 kg in five weeks. Compare like with like and the real change is 62.2 → 58.2 kg.

That still leaves a genuine −4.0 kg, spread as −2.2 kg torso, −1.4 kg legs, −0.4 kg arms, against −5.9 kg of fat over the same period. The written analysis covers what that mix means and how much of it is measurement noise.
